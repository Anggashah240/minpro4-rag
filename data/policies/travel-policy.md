# Travel Policy
## 1. Approval
Business travel must be approved by the employee's manager before booking. Personal upgrades are not reimbursable unless separately approved.
## 2. Transport
Economy airfare and approved ground transportation are reimbursable for business travel. Receipts are required according to the expense policy.
## 3. Accommodation
Standard hotel reimbursement is limited to **USD 150 per night, excluding taxes**. If no compliant hotel is reasonably available, the employee must obtain documented pre-approval for an exception.
## 4. Claim timing
Travel expenses follow the company expense-submission deadline. The travel policy does not define a different deadline.
## 5. Scope boundary
This policy does not specify future preferred hotel chains, executive exceptions, or future device models.
